\section{Protocol and deviations}
\label{app:protocol}

The study protocol was committed on 17 October 2026, before any result in this paper was
computed. It fixed the question, data snapshot, target, nested feature groups (C, D, D0, E, R180,
R90), fire-data latencies, model and hyperparameters (one LightGBM fit with fixed seed and 400
rounds for every variant), leave-one-year-out and operational evaluation, metrics, the block
bootstrap and hypotheses H1--H4. Changes made afterwards, in order:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{hit@2\% ties (17 Oct).} Binary predictors such as persistence produced ties that
        flagged more than 2\% of hours. Exactly $k$ hours are now alerted, ties broken at random.
        AP, ROC-AUC and Brier scores are unaffected.
  \item \emph{Persistent flow (17 Oct, exploratory).} Variants Ep and Rp180, using the 24\,h
        vector-mean wind. Single fits only.
  \item \emph{Shifted-wind placebo (18 Oct, after the first leave-one-year-out results).} Rshift
        and Rpshift (wind from 168\,h earlier), the logistic-regression family, and per-season
        mean $\Delta$AP; the missingness control D0m.
  \item \emph{Review round 1 (25 Oct, before computing them).} Wind-report categories (D0cat,
        Ecat), mask-preserving placebos (Rperm1--3, Rshiftm168), further shifts (72, 336,
        720\,h) with similarity diagnostics, vegetation-only fires (D0v, Ev, Rshiftv, D0mv),
        all-station bootstrap blocks, and logistic versions lgD0cat, lgEcat, lgRperm1.
  \item \emph{Seed ensembles (25 Oct, after item 4's results).} Single fits were found to vary
        strongly with the seed (Section~\ref{sec:seeds}). Main LightGBM results now use five-seed
        ensembles for C, D, D0, E, Rshift, Rperm1, D0m, D0cat, Ecat, D0v and Ev, and for the
        latency runs. Single-fit results are kept below.
\end{enumerate}

The decision rule for the directional claim did not change: direction counts as helping only if
E beats the placebos and the non-directional controls, not only D0.
